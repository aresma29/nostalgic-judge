Some childhoods survive in photographs, but mine lives in the kitchen air: steam rising, insects warming up for the night, and the quiet certainty that dinner was already on its way before I even knew I was hungry.

> Somewhere between the rice steaming on grandmother's stove and the evening cicadas tuning up outside, I learned that home was never a place but the smell of dinner arriving before I was hungry.
